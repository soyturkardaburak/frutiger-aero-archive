## A metaphor, not a shine

A skeuomorph keeps a feature of an old object after the construction no longer needs it. In software, the word settled on interfaces that imitate materials: a calendar with stitching, a bookshelf with wood grain, a game table in green felt, a notepad with yellow paper and a margin line. The imitation tells you what the program is for, by reminding you of a thing you have already held. CARI lists skeuomorphism among the motifs of Frutiger Aero. The overlap is real. The identity is not.

A glossy orb with a highlight is often just a glossy orb. It does not depict a particular tool. Aero Glass depicts glass, which is a material metaphor, and it also solves a compositing problem: you can see that another window is behind this one. Those are related pleasures. Only one of them is trying to be a desk accessory.

## Where it flourished

The first iPhone was announced in January 2007 and went on sale that June. Its early operating system, and the versions that followed until 2013, used texture as instruction. Notes looked like paper because notes were paper. The compass looked like a compass. This was a different studio from the one painting Windows frames, and a different argument. Apple was teaching a touch screen with objects. Microsoft was teaching a mouse desktop with a new surface. Both trusted that a computer in 2007 should feel physical. Flat design later called that trust embarrassing.

Web sites of the same years were less often skeuomorphic in the full sense. A reflected logo does not imitate a specific product so much as it imitates the idea of a product photograph. The encyclopedia keeps a separate article for that gloss.

## The break in 2013

iOS 7, released on 18 September 2013, is the public breakup. Apple took the heavy textures off the phone and left a brighter, flatter system that still had translucency in places but no longer asked the calendar to be leather. The argument, made by the software itself, was that people now knew what a phone application was. The metaphor could retire. Windows had already turned toward flat tiles in 2012. Skeuomorphism did not become impossible after that. It became a choice that read as nostalgic or as a joke, which is a hard fate for a teaching tool.

## How to use the word on this site

If a control imitates another object, call it skeuomorphic. If it is shiny, call it glossy. If the frame is a composited blur on Windows Vista or 7, call it Aero. Frutiger Aero is the later shelf on which all three sometimes sit together. The shelf is handy. The labels on the objects still matter.
